Why Tahoe, and why now

Lake Tahoe is known for its clear blue water. Out on the lake, a small research team measures the tiny particles that put that clarity at risk, and this film follows them while they work.

Quick facts

Curious about the research before you read the full story?

  • Water is collected at three depths: the surface, 5 meters, and 15 meters.
  • The team uses a LISST 200X to record particle size and count.
  • The research is funded by NOAA, with the Desert Research Institute as a partner.
